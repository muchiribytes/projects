Discover the Flavors of Thailand

Tom Yum Goong is a classic Thai soup renowned for its bold flavors and aromatic spices. This hot and sour soup features a tantalizing blend of lemongrass, galangal, and kaffir lime leaves, combined with succulent shrimp.

Preparation time

  • Total: Approximately 20 minutes
  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Cooking: 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 stalks Lemongrass
  • 1 cup Thai basil
  • 3 leaves Kaffir Lime Leaves
  • 500g Shrimp
  • Optional seasonings: fish sauce, lime juice, chili paste

Instructions

  1. Simmer the broth: Bring broth to a low boil for 10 minutes. You can add a splash of fish sauce for extra flavor depth.
  2. Add aromatics: Place lemongrass, galangal, and kaffir lime leaves into the pot over medium heat.
  3. Cook the shrimp: Once boiling, pour in the shrimp. Stir gently to ensure even cooking.
  4. Season to taste: When edges set, adjust chili paste and lime juice to reach desired heat.
  5. Garnish and serve: Top with fresh Thai basil leaves and slide into serving bowls.
  6. Enjoy: Serve hot, with extra chili or jasmine rice if needed.
